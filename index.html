Dr. Sandesh Mayekar

About the Speaker

Dr. Sandesh Mayekar is recognized as the Father of Modern Dentistry in India. He founded the Indian Academy of Aesthetic & Cosmetic Dentistry and has been a Consultant for Miss India Smiles for over 30 years.

Dr. Ganesh Jeevanandan

About the Speaker

Dr. Ganesh Jeevanandan is a Professor in the Department of Pediatric & Preventive Dentistry at Saveetha Dental College & Hospitals, Chennai. He is the Founder of KEDO Dental and inventor of Kedo-S rotary files.

Dr. Pradeep S.

About the Speaker

Dr. Pradeep S. is a Professor in the Department of Conservative & Endodontics at Saveetha Dental College & Hospitals, Chennai. His academic and clinical work includes rotary endodontics in permanent teeth.

Dr. Rama Moondra

About the Speaker

Dr. Rama Moondra is the Dean of the Adani Institute of Digital Technology Management. Her NxtGen Dentistry 2026 session explores Healthcare 2.0 and the AI revolution.
